## CALLER SLICES

After the function comes an `UNTRUSTED_CONTEXT_CALLER_SLICES` block from the repository's call
graph: for each call, only the caller's lines its arguments depend on, the if/while/for/try lines
around them, the call itself, and the caller parameters that feed it (values from further up, any
value compatible with their use). Use it to decide which values reach the parameters: does the
caller already filter or convert them? No block means no call was found. Do not report findings
about the callers' code.
